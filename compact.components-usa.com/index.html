About Us.

Components USA was established in historic Salem, MA in 2008 and is driven by 33 years of sourcing experience.

We supply active and passive electronic components from commercial grade to full military level. We also have expertise in connectors, switches, wire and cable. You can expect quick action from us which can result with parts on your dock in 24 to 48 hours in critical situations.
